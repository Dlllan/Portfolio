About me

Hello my name is Dylan! Driven by a passion for transforming raw data into actionable insights, I am currently pursuing a Data Analyst degree at Saint Paul College, building a robust foundation in IT, with a strong emphasis on CSS, HTML, SQL, and Python. My technical skills are complemented by a keen analytical mind honed through four years of management experience at CVS, where I navigated complex retail and pharmacy operations, fostering efficient workflows and data-driven decision-making. Outside of academics, I indulge in the creative precision of 3D printing, the immersive worlds of literature, and the dynamic energy of sports, all of which contribute to a well-rounded perspective that I bring to problem-solving and data analysis.

This summer, I'm laying the groundwork for advanced data analysis with an Introduction to Statistics course. In the fall, I'll build upon that foundation with Intermediate Statistics and Introduction to Data Science, furthering my pursuit of a Data Analyst degree. Balancing academic rigor with professional commitment, I'm a full-time employee at CVS Health, where I'm recognized for my accountability and collaborative spirit, consistently delivering positive outcomes for both colleagues and customers/patients.
